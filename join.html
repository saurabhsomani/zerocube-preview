You join as a Global Climate Citizen

वसुधैव कुटुम्बकम् Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam: the world is one family. Every member is a citizen of one planetary family, with responsibilities to the planet and to each other. Global citizenship education is part of SDG target 4.7.

What happens next

  1. Tell us who you are, your country and how you would like to contribute
  2. We write to you personally, and invite you to free webinars and courses straight away
  3. Pay the fee for your way of belonging by bank transfer, UPI or online (how to pay) and send us the payment reference
  4. We email your receipt and welcome, and invite you to members-only sessions
Zero Cube is a not-for-profit (Section 8) foundation registered in Pune, India. Our field work starts in Indian villages, schools and small enterprises, and the learning, the ideas and the community are open to the whole world. Fees are shown in Indian rupees (INR), because Zero Cube is registered in India. People outside India take part through membership fees only (no donations) and are asked to write to us before paying from abroad. Anyone, anywhere can learn free and take part online.
From outside India? You are very welcome as a member. People outside India take part through membership fees only; Zero Cube does not accept donations from foreign nationals or foreign sources. Please write to us before paying, so that we can confirm the right way to pay from abroad. FCRA position: CS/CA to confirm

Programme members are participants and supporters, not members of the company under the Companies Act, 2013. Membership terms · Privacy
