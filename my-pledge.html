How the login works

  1. 1Type your email. For your privacy we always say “if a pledge exists, we've sent a link”.
  2. 2Open the email from zerocubecif@gmail.com and tap the link. It works once and expires in 15 minutes.
  3. 3You stay logged in on that device for 7 days, or until you log out.

No passwords are stored. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy of each link. Not pledged yet? Take the Pledge.

Withdraw my pledge

You can withdraw at any time (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). We remove you from the Wall of Honour and erase your details, photo and documents. The fingerprint stays in the Zero Cube Pledge Ledger, but it can no longer be linked to you; your withdrawal is recorded in a later block and your pledge ID shows “Withdrawn”.
