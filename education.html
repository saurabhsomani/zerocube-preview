Quality Education

Education is a basic, fundamental right. Every child must get an equal opportunity to learn — irrespective of gender, caste, religion, income, location or disability.

In one line

Every child has an equal right to good education — and should learn to care for the climate.

Our belief

An equal right for every child

India's Constitution makes free and compulsory education for children aged 6 to 14 a fundamental right (Article 21A, inserted in 2002), given effect by the Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009. The National Education Policy 2020 puts equitable and inclusive education at its heart, with special attention to socio-economically disadvantaged groups, and the UN's Sustainable Development Goal 4 calls for inclusive, equitable quality education for all.

We add one more dimension: education should also teach every child their moral responsibility towards the climate and all living beings. Government and low-fee schools never pay for our programmes; sponsors fund them.

Where India stands

14.7 lakhschools (UDISE+ 2024-25)
24.69 crorestudents enrolled (UDISE+ 2024-25)

Access has grown, but learning still lags: in ASER 2024 (rural), 23.4% of Std III children in government schools could read a Std II-level text — the highest since ASER began, yet most children still need support.

What equal opportunity means to us

  • No child left out because of gender, caste, religion, income, location or disability
  • Programmes free for government and low-fee schools
  • Foundational learning alongside climate literacy
  • Inclusive materials and accessible formats

Our commitments

  • Public webinars are free for everyone
  • We issue Zero Cube certificates and badges only
  • Written school consent and parental consent for any child data or photo
  • A child-safeguarding policy governs every school programme
Schools

School programme

Carbon Detectives

Classes 6–12: a term-long project in which students audit their school's electricity, waste, water and transport.

Net Zero School pathway & assessments

Campus inventory, curriculum links and student action, with independent review before any certificate. Sliding-scale fees; government schools free.

Climate Teacher Fellowship

A six-month fellowship for teachers in government and low-fee schools, with mentor and classroom kit, funded by donors and sponsors.

Institution / School membership

Members-only sessions and campus roles; Academy courses are free for everyone.
