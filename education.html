Quality and Equal Education

Every child on Earth deserves the same quality education. Inequality begins in the first classroom; we want it to end there.

In one line

Every child has an equal right to good education — and should learn to care for the climate.

  • 4Quality Education
  • 5Gender Equality
  • 10Reduced Inequalities
  • 17Partnerships for the Goals

Especially SDG targets 4.1 (free, equitable, quality primary and secondary education), 4.5 (eliminate disparities), 4.7 (global citizenship and sustainable development), 4.a (safe, inclusive schools) and 4.c (qualified teachers). Zero Cube is an independent foundation; SDG references show alignment only and do not imply UN endorsement.

Our dream

Every child on Earth deserves the same quality education.

Today, where a child is born still decides the school they walk into, the teacher who stands in front of them and the doors that open after. We dream of a world where every child, in every village and every city, gets the same quality education, the same equal chance, and grows up to look after the planet and each other.

This is not only about climate lessons. It is about the whole of a child’s education being equal: the school, the teaching and the chances that follow. Climate responsibility is part of that education, not a replacement for it.

Our education dream

One ecosystem around every child

Three connected parts, each as important as the others. When all three are equal for every child, the child’s own dream can lead the way.

  1. 1

    Equal school infrastructure for every child

    A safe school building, clean water and toilets, light and electricity, books, labs and digital access. Not a better school for some children and a worse one for others: the same strong foundation for all.

    • SDG 4.a
    • SDG 4.1
  2. 2

    Teachers who shape responsible citizens in every classroom

    Valued, well-supported teachers who help every child learn well, and who grow responsible, climate-aware global citizens who care for the planet and for each other.

    • SDG 4.c
    • SDG 4.7
  3. 3

    Industry and society around every child

    Companies through CSR, mentors, professionals and communities who open doors: skills, guidance and real-world opportunities, so each child can grow and give back.

    • SDG 4.4
    • SDG 17.17

  4. Every child’s dream

    Whatever they dream of becoming, and whatever they want to contribute to society.

Frameworks to see the gaps, and to close them

Zero Cube is designing open frameworks that help schools, communities and supporters measure the quality and equity of education across all three parts: the school a child learns in, the teaching they receive, and the support around them. Each framework will show where the gaps are, so that CSR, volunteers and communities can close them where they matter most.

Our education frameworks are being designed. We will publish draft criteria openly for comment before they are used; no school has been assessed or certified under them yet.

Our belief

An equal right for every child

India's Constitution makes free and compulsory education for children aged 6 to 14 a fundamental right (Article 21A, inserted in 2002), given effect by the Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009. The National Education Policy 2020 puts equitable and inclusive education at its heart, with special attention to socio-economically disadvantaged groups, and the UN's Sustainable Development Goal 4 calls for inclusive, equitable quality education for all.

We add one more dimension: education should also teach every child their moral responsibility towards the climate and all living beings. Government and low-fee schools never pay for our programmes; sponsors fund them.

Where India stands

14.7 lakhschools (UDISE+ 2024-25)
24.69 crorestudents enrolled (UDISE+ 2024-25)

Access has grown, but learning still lags: in ASER 2024 (rural), 23.4% of Std III children in government schools could read a Std II-level text — the highest since ASER began, yet most children still need support.

What equal opportunity means to us

  • No child left out because of gender, caste, religion, income, location or disability
  • Programmes free for government and low-fee schools
  • Equal school infrastructure, valued teachers and real-world support for every child
  • Foundational learning alongside climate literacy
  • Inclusive materials and accessible formats

Our commitments

  • Public webinars are free for everyone
  • We issue Zero Cube certificates and badges only
  • Written school consent and parental consent for any child data or photo
  • A child-safeguarding policy governs every school programme
Frameworks in development

What our education frameworks are being designed to look at

For each part of the ecosystem, the framework asks simple questions a school, a community or a supporter can answer honestly, and shows where help is needed first.

Infrastructure

Is the school safe and accessible for every child, including girls and children with disabilities? Are there clean water, toilets, light, books and digital access? SDG 4.a

Teaching

Do teachers have the training, time, materials and support they need? Is every child learning, and learning to be a responsible global citizen? SDG 4.c, 4.7

Industry and society

Do children meet mentors and see real careers? Are CSR, volunteers and the community helping close the gaps the school cannot close alone? SDG 4.4, 17.17

Equity

Are the gaps between children, schools and places getting smaller? Every result is read through equity first. SDG 4.5, 10

Our education frameworks are being designed. We will publish draft criteria openly for comment before they are used; no school has been assessed or certified under them yet.
