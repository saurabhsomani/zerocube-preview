Pledge methodology

How every number in the Zero Cube Pledge is calculated.

In one line

How every pledge number is calculated — published factors, cautious inputs, rounded down.

Plain words
GHG Protocol
the common way to count greenhouse gases

Methodology v1 (October 2026) — to be reviewed by Zero Cube's expert panel. Indicative estimates for awareness — not measured, verified or certified reductions, not carbon credits, and not an assessment or certification of any person.

Principles

  1. Before vs afterFollowing the idea of the GHG Protocol Policy and Action Standard, we estimate the change a habit makes against what you did before. Count only what you will newly do, or do more of.
  2. Published factorsIndian factors first (CEA, India GHG Program, BEE, MNRE, CPCB, CPHEEO). Where India has none, we use UK DESNZ 2025, FAO, UNEP, IPCC or US DOE/EPA, and say so.
  3. Cautious and rounded downWe pick the low end of each range, round factors down and round each habit down to whole kg.
  4. No double countingEach habit has its own inputs. Teaching others counts people reached, not their CO₂e.

Habit by habit

I will not use the AC more than 3 hours a day, and I will set it to 24–26 °C

Home energy

Your inputs (defaults)

  • Hours of AC I will cut a day: 2 h (range 0–12)
  • Degrees I will raise the setting: 2 °C (range 0–4)
  • Months a year I use AC: 4 months (range 1–12)

Formula

0.75 kWh × (months × 30) × (hours cut + 3 h × 6% × °C raised) × 0.71

Factors used

  • Small efficient AC (1 ton) power use: 0.75 kWh per hour of AC
  • Saving for each 1 °C higher setting: 0.06 share of AC electricity per °C
  • AC hours still used (the pledge limit): 3 hours a day
  • Indian grid electricity: 0.71 kg CO₂ per kWh

With the defaults

Saves about 212 units of electricity a year — ≈150 kg CO₂e a year

Indicative estimate, rounded down to whole kg.

Quality education pledge

Educators

For “Teacher / Educator / Professor / Education professional” — and for all under-18s (peer tutoring):

Students reached = students I will personally teach or mentor (at least 1)
Learning hours a year = hours a month × 12

Everyone else

Financial support, given directly to the student or school, or through a Verified Projects listing:

₹ a year = students (at least 1) × ₹ per student per year

Examples shown on the form (₹1,500 school kit & books, ₹3,000 digital learning, ₹12,000 scholarship) are editable examples, not costs or prices set by Zero Cube. Zero Cube does not collect this money. No tax benefit is implied.

What we do not count

  • Trees after year 3 (they absorb more as they grow, but survival varies).
  • An electric car on today’s grid (about the same CO₂ per km as a small petrol car).
  • The CO₂e of people you teach — only their own pledges would count.
  • Health, money and air-quality benefits — real, but not added to CO₂e.
  • Green power tariffs (they vary by state).

Server-side check

The same factor table runs in the browser and on our pledge server. When a pledge is submitted, the server recalculates CO₂e, ₹ and learning hours from your inputs, so the Wall of Honour always uses the same method.
