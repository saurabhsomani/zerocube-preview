Founders' track record

Our founders' prior work: 34 Net Zero certificates (1 Gram Panchayat, 33 clubs) issued in 2025-26 in association with Rotary District 3131's Environment Team. This work was done before Zero Cube Climate & Social Impact Foundation was incorporated on 28 September 2026, so these are not Zero Cube's own certificates or results, and no current partnership or MoU with Rotary is implied.

Zero Cube's own results will be reported here as our programmes begin, with each certificate naming its criteria version, boundary and validity.
