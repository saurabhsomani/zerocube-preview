What “philanthropist” means at Zero Cube

  • Time, expertise and networks — not money. Our directors give their time, expertise and networks voluntarily and receive no salary, sitting fee or other remuneration. Directors do not fund the company: no director loans, guarantees or liability, and spending follows income.
  • Membership or donations are voluntary. A director may take any Zero Cube membership tier at the normal fee (no special discount unless the Board approves it openly) or make a voluntary donation. This is never a condition of being a director, is not a loan, and gives no liability or control.
  • Disclosed and reviewed. Any director membership or donation is recorded in the Board’s register of interests and in the minutes, and is reviewed by our Company Secretary and Chartered Accountant.
  • Nothing comes back. Donations go only to the Foundation’s objects, or directly to beneficiaries. No director receives any benefit or preferential service in return. A director’s personal giving to beneficiaries is separate and direct, and never passes through Zero Cube.
