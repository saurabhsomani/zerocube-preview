Donate · From the people, for the people

Donate

Your gift keeps climate learning free for everyone, helps every child get a quality, equal education, and helps villages go Net Zero without paying a rupee.

In one line

Your gift keeps learning free, helps every child learn and helps villages go Net Zero. Indian donors only; receipts from the Foundation.

Where your gift goes

Applied only to our charitable objects

As a not-for-profit Section 8 company, every rupee is applied only to promoting our objects. No dividend or distribution is ever made, and our directors serve without remuneration.

Free learning for all

Public webinars and Climate Academy courses, free for everyone, everywhere.

Quality and equal education

School programmes and frameworks so every child gets the same quality education.

NetZero villages

Energy, water, trees, waste and livelihoods. Villages never pay.

Where most needed

A general gift lets the Board apply it where it does the most good.

You can name a purpose when you send us your payment details; restricted gifts are applied to that purpose. See where every contribution goes and our donation policy.

Who can donate

  • Indian citizens and Indian organisations, from a bank account in India, in Indian rupees.
  • Foreign nationals and foreign sources: we do not accept donations. You are very welcome to join as a member instead (membership fees only).
  • Indian citizens living abroad: please write to us before giving.

FCRA position: CS/CA to confirm

Tax benefit

80G: our application for 12A/80G registration is pending. Until it is approved, donations are not eligible for an income-tax deduction. Tax-exemption (80G) receipts will be available only after approval, for donations on or after its effective date. CS/CA to confirm
