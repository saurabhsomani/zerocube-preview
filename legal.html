Not-for-profit rules we follow

  • Our objects are charitable: climate action, environment, education and social impact. We act only within the objects in our Memorandum of Association.
  • All income, property and any surplus are applied only to promoting those objects.
  • No dividend, bonus or other distribution is paid, directly or indirectly, to members of the company or to directors.
  • Our directors serve without remuneration. Members of the company receive no remuneration or other benefit beyond what the law and our Articles permit.
  • Any change to our Memorandum or Articles needs the Registrar’s prior approval.

Programme members are not company members

People and organisations who join our membership programme are participants and supporters of our charitable work. They are not members of the company under the Companies Act, 2013, and get no shares, ownership, voting or profit rights. Membership fees are contributions applied wholly to our objects; the benefits are programme services. See the membership terms.

What we do not claim

  • We do not claim any tax exemption, government approval or government endorsement.
  • Zero Cube certificates are programme certificates under our own published criteria, not government licences, accreditation or carbon credits.
  • We name organisations as partners only after a written agreement is signed.
  • Zero Cube is an independent foundation; SDG references show alignment only and do not imply UN endorsement.
  • Founders’ prior work (34 Net Zero certificates in 2025-26 with Rotary District 3131’s Environment Team) was done before incorporation and is not presented as Zero Cube’s own results.

Related parties and conflicts

Dealings with any entity related to a director are disclosed to the Board, recorded in the register of interests and approved without the interested director. We do not promote any director’s business on this website.
