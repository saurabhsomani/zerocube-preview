Terms of use

The simple rules for using this website and taking part in our programme.

Draft, under review by our CS/CA Last updated 21 October 2026.

Using this website

Content on this site is provided for information and education. Certificates, reports and figures are valid only as described in their published scope. Links to other sites are not endorsements. Please ask us in writing before using our name or marks.

Programme membership terms

  • Participants, not company members. Members of the Zero Cube programme are participants and supporters of our charitable work. They are not members of the company under the Companies Act, 2013, and get no shares, ownership, voting, dividend or profit rights. Company membership is separate and is admitted by the Board.
  • Fees are contributions to the objects. Membership fees are applied wholly to promoting our objects. The benefits described for each way of belonging are programme services that support our charitable work, not a commercial product.
  • No tax benefit. Membership fees are not donations and are not eligible for 80G.
  • Nothing to pay now. We are not accepting any payment until our bank account is active. When membership opens, fees will be shown in Indian rupees (INR).
  • International participants. Anyone, anywhere can join the waitlist, learn free and take part online. We cannot accept foreign payments or donations until the applicable approvals, including FCRA registration, are in place.
  • Independence. No fee, membership or sponsorship buys a certificate, an approval or an endorsement.
  • Conduct. Members follow our code of conduct. Membership can be paused or ended for serious breaches.
  • Proposed tiers. Some ways of belonging and their benefits are proposed and subject to Board approval.

Liability

We take care to keep this site accurate, but it is provided as is. Nothing here is legal, tax or financial advice.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India; courts at Pune have jurisdiction.
