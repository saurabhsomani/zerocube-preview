Membership · From the people, for the people

Ways to belong

Membership at Zero Cube is not a product. It is how you belong to a people’s movement, as a Global Climate Citizen, and help create change: as a student, teacher, professional, school, enterprise, NPO, village or company, from anywhere in the world.

In one line

Belong in your own way, as a Global Climate Citizen, from anywhere in the world. Each way of belonging starts with the change you help create.

Global Climate Citizen

Every member is a citizen of one planetary family

Whichever way you belong, you join as a Global Climate Citizen: someone who accepts a responsibility to the planet and to each other, learns and shares freely, and acts where they live. वसुधैव कुटुम्बकम् Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam: the world is one family.

  • SDG 4
  • SDG 13
  • SDG 17

Global citizenship education is part of SDG target 4.7. Zero Cube is an independent foundation; SDG references show alignment only and do not imply UN endorsement.

What “member” means here

Members of this programme are participants and supporters of Zero Cube’s charitable work. They are not members of the company under the Companies Act, 2013, and get no shares, ownership, voting or profit rights. Membership fees are contributions applied wholly to our objects; the benefits are programme services, not a commercial product. Membership terms · Company membership

Learning is free

Learn, then participate, then act

All our webinars and Climate Academy online courses are free for everyone — no membership needed. Membership supports deeper programme work, never access to knowledge.

  1. 1

    Learn — free for all

    Public webinars on Net Zero, SROI and education, and self-paced Climate Academy courses. Open to everyone, always free.

  2. 2

    Participate — as a member

    Hands-on roles, members-only sessions twice a month, field work, campus chapters and online networking with other members.

  3. 3

    Act — and be recognised

    Apply for your Commitment Certificate, register interest in a Net Zero audit or assessment, and earn recognition for what you do.

What your membership makes possible

  • Hands-on roles and members-only sessions
  • The Commitment Certificate, with its own ledger entry (no extra fee)
  • Audits and assessments, as per the fee card
  • Networking with members across sectors
  • Recognition: badges, certificates and the opt-in Wall of Honour

Not for knowledge: every webinar and Climate Academy course stays free for everyone.

How certification works

AI-first, then a human verifier

Every membership unlocks the Commitment Certificate application and Net Zero audit interest. Here is how we check what you submit.

1 · AI-first review

Your pledge, data and evidence are first checked by our AI tools for completeness and consistency.

2 · Human verifier

A trained Zero Cube verifier then reviews the result and decides. Nothing is certified by AI alone.

3 · Optional independent verification

If you want third-party assurance, an independent verification body (accredited under ISO 14065) can verify your claim. This is an optional add-on paid directly to the verifier by MSMEs, or through CSR for villages. It never increases your membership fee.

Standards we follow

  • GHG Protocol (corporate and value-chain / Scope 3)
  • ISO 14064 (quantifying, reporting and verifying greenhouse gases)
  • ISO 14068 (carbon neutrality: reduce first)
  • dMRV: digital measurement, reporting and verification

What the Commitment Certificate means

It certifies your commitment, not results or verified emission cuts. Each certificate is verifiable through its own entry on the Zero Cube Pledge Ledger, at no extra fee. “Zero Cube assessed” means assessed under our own criteria; it is not a third-party certification unless an independent verifier has also verified it.

Villages never pay

Village Net Zero certification is funded through CSR. A village or Gram Panchayat needs no membership for it, and is never charged. Certified clubs and Gram Panchayats may also join the network free as a Certified Network Member.

CSR funds go to programmes such as village certification. CSR cannot be used to pay membership fees.

Board members and membership

Our Board are philanthropists of time, expertise and networks and draw no pay. A director may also hold a membership at the normal published fee, or donate voluntarily. Every such membership or donation is disclosed and recorded, is never a condition of being a director, and gives no benefit in return.
