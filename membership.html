Membership FAQ

Do I pay extra for the audit, dMRV or certification?

No. In 2026-27, audit, dMRV and certification are included in your membership. No extra cost.

Is Zero Cube an accredited verifier?

No. Zero Cube certificates are programme certificates under our own published criteria, aligned with ISO 14068:2026 and the GHG Protocol. They are not accredited third-party verification, government licences or carbon credits.

Who never pays?

Villages never pay (CSR and donors fund them). Children’s education is free and unconditional. Webinars and courses are free for everyone.

What “member” means here

Members of this programme are participants and supporters of Zero Cube’s charitable work. They are not members of the company under the Companies Act, 2013, and get no shares, ownership, voting or profit rights. Membership fees are contributions applied wholly to our objects; the benefits are programme services, not a commercial product. Membership terms · Company membership

AI-first, then a human verifier
How certification works

AI-first, then a human verifier

Every membership unlocks the Commitment Certificate application and Net Zero audit interest. Here is how we check what you submit.

1 · AI-first review

Your pledge, data and evidence are first checked by our AI tools for completeness and consistency.

2 · Human verifier

A trained Zero Cube verifier then reviews the result and decides. Nothing is certified by AI alone.

3 · Optional independent verification

If you want third-party assurance, an independent verification body (accredited under ISO 14065) can verify your claim. This is an optional add-on paid directly to the verifier by MSMEs, or through CSR for villages. It never increases your membership fee.

Standards we align with

  • GHG Protocol (corporate and value-chain / Scope 3)
  • ISO 14064 (quantifying, reporting and verifying greenhouse gases)
  • Aligned with ISO 14068:2026 and the GHG Protocol (carbon neutrality: reduce first)
  • dMRV: digital measurement, reporting and verification

What the Commitment Certificate means

It certifies your commitment, not results or verified emission cuts. Each certificate is verifiable through its own entry on the Zero Cube Pledge Ledger, at no extra fee. “Zero Cube assessed” means assessed under our own criteria; it is not a third-party certification unless an independent verifier has also verified it.
