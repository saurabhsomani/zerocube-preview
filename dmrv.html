How we deliver

dMRV & Impact Portal

Digital measurement, reporting and verification — the evidence behind our SROI reports and Net Zero certificates.

In one line

Satellite and digital data prove what changed on the ground.

One membership. Everything included. No hidden or extra costs.In 2026-27, audit, dMRV and certification are included in your membership. No extra cost. All membership and sponsorship fees go only to our work; most goes to dMRV and expert reviewers. Framework support, SROI reports for NPOs, Scope 3 support, programmes and trainings are included too. Always free: villages never pay, children’s education, and webinars and courses for everyone.

Human-in-the-loop: every satellite or app result is reviewed by a trained human expert before any certificate is issued.

Satellite handprint

Green cover, surviving trees, water bodies and canopy change, measured over time.

AI-assisted footprint

Footprint tools that estimate emissions in tonnes CO2e by source.

Reports & certificates

Published reports and certificates that NPOs and companies can share with donors and use in ESG reporting.

Separate numbers

Handprint is never netted against footprint unless it meets the criteria and is independently verified.

The Impact Portal

One data system serving both main goals and our SROI work: SROI uses its outcome data, certification uses its evidence, and education uses its cases. Dashboards show footprint, handprint and SROI for each project, with export files for CSR and ESG reporting.

Who uses it

  • CSR teams and donors tracking funded projects
  • NPOs reporting on Verified Projects
  • Villages and schools on the Net Zero pathway
  • Members reviewing impact reports
