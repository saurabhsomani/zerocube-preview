Where your fees go

All membership and sponsorship fees are used only for our work: audits, dMRV, the human experts who check every result, certification, the AI Launchpad and education programmes, plus the compliance needed to run them. Most goes to dMRV and expert reviewers. Directors take no salary.

  • Always free: villages never pay · children’s education is free and unconditional · webinars and courses are free for everyone.
  • From outside India? People outside India take part through membership fees only (no donations). Please write to us before paying from abroad.
  • Our certificates are programme certificates under our own published criteria, aligned with ISO 14068:2026 and the GHG Protocol. They are not accredited third-party verification, government licences or carbon credits.

How to pay Our promises in full

Programme members are participants and supporters, not members of the company: no shares, ownership, voting or profit rights. Membership terms

Membership FAQ

Do I pay extra for the audit, dMRV or certification?

No. In 2026-27, audit, dMRV and certification are included in your membership. No extra cost.

Is Zero Cube an accredited verifier?

No. Zero Cube certificates are programme certificates under our own published criteria, aligned with ISO 14068:2026 and the GHG Protocol. They are not accredited third-party verification, government licences or carbon credits.

Can our school join the AI Launchpad?

Yes. Any school we audit can opt in for its Std 8-10 students. It is included in your membership and free for every student in Std 8-10.

Who never pays?

Villages never pay (CSR and donors fund them). Children’s education is free and unconditional. Webinars and courses are free for everyone.
