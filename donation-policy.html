Donation policy

How we accept, use and account for every gift.

Draft, under review by our CS/CA Last updated 24 October 2026. CS/CA to confirm

Who can donate

  • We accept donations only from Indian citizens and Indian organisations, in Indian rupees, from bank accounts in India.
  • We do not accept donations from foreign nationals or any foreign source. People outside India can take part through membership fees only. FCRA position: CS/CA to confirm
  • Indian citizens living abroad are asked to write to us before giving.
  • We do not accept cash, or gifts that come with conditions against our objects, our independence or the law. We may decline any gift.

How to give

By bank transfer, UPI or online payment, only to the account in the Foundation’s name shown on the Donate page. We never ask anyone to pay into a personal account.

How gifts are used

  • Every donation is applied only to promoting our objects as a Section 8 company: no dividend or distribution, and directors serve without remuneration.
  • Restricted gifts: if you name a purpose (for example free learning, education or NetZero villages), we apply your gift to that purpose. If that is no longer possible, we write to you before using it for another of our objects.
  • Villages never pay for our work; donations and CSR help make that possible.
  • Our accounts are audited every year, as the law requires.

Receipts and tax

  • Receipts are issued by ZERO CUBE CLIMATE & SOCIAL IMPACT FOUNDATION (CIN U88900PN2026NPL260913, PAN AADCZ1959P) and emailed within [7] working days of the funds reaching our account.
  • 80G: our application for 12A/80G registration is pending. Until it is approved, donations are not eligible for an income-tax deduction. Tax-exemption (80G) receipts will be available only after approval, for donations on or after its effective date. CS/CA to confirm
  • To receive a receipt, please send us your name, address, email, amount, date and transaction reference (and PAN, which will be needed for 80G reporting once approved).

Recognition and privacy

  • Donors are thanked publicly only with their consent; you can give anonymously.
  • A donation never buys a certificate, an approval, an endorsement or a place on any list.
  • Your details are used only for receipts, statutory records and to thank you; see our privacy policy.

CSR

CSR contributions are not donations under this policy. They are made under a written agreement, after our CSR-1 registration and eligibility are confirmed. See CSR partnerships.

Refunds

Donations made in error can be refunded on written request within 15 days: see the refund & cancellation policy.
