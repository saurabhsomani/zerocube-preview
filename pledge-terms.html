Pledge terms & privacy

Draft — CS/legal to confirm before the pledge database goes live.

In one line

Draft rules for the pledge: one lifelong pledge, voluntary, consent-based, and you can withdraw your details any time.

Draft — CS/legal to confirm. This text is for review and is not yet in force.

1. What the pledge is

The Zero Cube Pledge is a free, voluntary moral commitment made by you, for you. You take it once, for the rest of your life (“Climate Duty Bearer”, for life). It is your personal responsibility. It is not a legal contract, not a donation, not a membership, and gives no tax benefit. Zero Cube does not monitor or enforce it. Numbers shown are indicative estimates under our Pledge methodology, not measured or verified reductions.

1A. One pledge per person

Each person may hold one pledge. We use your email (and mobile number, if you give it) only to check for a pledge already made; a second pledge is refused with a link to your existing profile. Being lifelong does not limit your rights: you can still withdraw consent and have your details deleted at any time (see section 5).

1B. Education pledge

Educators pledge the students they will personally teach or mentor and the hours. Others pledge financial support for students, given directly to the student or school, or through an NPO project on our Verified Projects listing. Zero Cube does not collect or hold this money; the pledge is not a payment or donation to Zero Cube. Example amounts are examples only.

2. Who runs it

Zero Cube Climate & Social Impact Foundation, a Section 8 not-for-profit company (CIN U88900PN2026NPL260913), registered office: PL9 SN 121/122 F21 RADHAK, NR STATE BANK PAUD, Ex. Serviceman Colony, Pune City, Pune 411038, Maharashtra, India. Contact: zerocubecif@gmail.com.

3. What we collect and why

  • Shown publicly on the Wall of Honour (only with your consent): name, city, state, type, chosen habits and their outcomes, your education pledge (students, and ₹ a year or learning hours), your words and pledge letter; photo only with separate photo consent.
  • Kept private: email and optional mobile number (to stop duplicate pledges and to confirm or withdraw your pledge), the small habit inputs you entered, age group, guardian name and consent for under-18s, consent records and date.
  • Not collected: address, payment or bank details. Your pledge card and letter are made in your browser and are not uploaded unless you submit.

4. Consent (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

We process your personal data on the basis of your free, specific, informed and unambiguous consent, given by ticking clear boxes, as the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 requires. Wall of Honour display and photo display are separate consents. You can give or refuse each one.

5. Withdrawal and deletion

You may withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Use “Request withdrawal” in My Pledge, or email zerocubecif@gmail.com from the address you used. We will remove you from the Wall of Honour and delete your photo, documents and data (including the salt of your ledger fingerprint — see 7A) from our records within a reasonable time [CS/legal to set, e.g. 7 working days], except where the law requires us to keep a record. You can also ask to correct your data.

6. Children (under 18)

Under-18s may pledge only with verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian. We do not show photos of under-18s, do not upload them, and do not use children's data for tracking or targeted advertising.

7. Moderation

Every pledge is reviewed before it appears. We may decline or remove content that is offensive, misleading, impersonates someone, promotes a business or breaks the law. Do not upload a photo of anyone else.

7A. Zero Cube Pledge Ledger (tamper-evident fingerprint)

When your pledge is approved, we make a pledge record file (your pledge plus a random secret number called a “salt”) and calculate its SHA-256 fingerprint. Each night, the fingerprints approved that day are added as a new block to the Zero Cube Pledge Ledger: a permissioned, append-only blockchain run by Zero Cube. Each block links to the previous block's hash and is digitally signed by Zero Cube, and the blocks are published openly so anyone can check that nothing has been changed. No third-party network or fees are involved.

  • No personal data goes in the ledger: no name, email, phone, photo or pledge text. Only the fingerprint, the pledge ID and the date.
  • Ledger blocks are never edited or deleted; a correction or withdrawal is recorded in a new block.
  • You receive your pledge record file by email and in My Pledge. Anyone holding it can check it on the Verify page, so share it only if you wish.
  • If you withdraw, we erase your data and the salt and record the withdrawal in a new block. The old fingerprint stays but can no longer be linked to you, and your pledge ID shows “Withdrawn”.

7B. Login and emails

To log in to My Pledge we email you a one-time link that expires in 15 minutes. We store only a scrambled (hashed) copy of login links and sessions, never passwords. We send you emails about your pledge only: receipt, approval with your certificate, letter and pledge record file, login links, Commitment Certificate and Net Zero audit interest updates, and withdrawal confirmation. Emails are sent from zerocubecif@gmail.com through Google's services [email provider(s) — CS/legal to confirm and list as data processors].

7C. Commitment Certificate (member benefit)

Taking the pledge is free for everyone. The Climate Duty Bearer Commitment Certificate is a benefit for Zero Cube members: you may apply in My Pledge when your pledge is approved and recorded in the Zero Cube Pledge Ledger and your membership is active (any membership tier, paid or free, including Student, Certified Network Member, Expert / Volunteer Associate and Patron). If you are already a member, you can ask us to link your membership by email match or member ID; a team member confirms it. There is no extra fee for the certificate. Our team checks your pledge and its ledger record (status: Applied, Under review, Issued) and may decline an application that does not meet our criteria, with a reason. The certificate certifies your commitment (your pledge) and that its record is unchanged. It does not certify achieved results, measured or verified emission reductions, carbon credits or an audit, and it is not third-party assurance or accredited verification. When it is issued we make a certificate record (with a random secret “salt”) and record only its number and fingerprint in the ledger, never your name. Signatures shown are [signatory and signature method — CS/legal to confirm]. Optional progress updates you add are self-declared: we do not check them, they are not printed on the certificate and they are not shown publicly; they may be used for a later renewal or upgrade [renewal period and criteria — to be decided]. If your membership expires, a certificate already issued stays valid as a record of your commitment, but renewal, upgrades, progress updates and new requests need an active membership [CS/legal to confirm]. If you withdraw your pledge, we erase your certificate details and salt; the fingerprint stays but can no longer be linked to you.

7D. Net Zero audit interest (member benefit)

When your pledge is approved and on the ledger and your membership is active, you may tell us in My Pledge you are interested in a Zero Cube Net Zero audit (an assessment under Zero Cube's own criteria) for your home, school, institution, MSME, village, club or housing society. This is only an expression of interest: it is not a booking, an order or a payment, and nothing is agreed until you accept a written scope. Villages never pay and never need a paid membership: a village or Gram Panchayat audit interest goes through the free CSR-funded route, with no membership needed; for others, fees are as per our published fee card. We use what you send (who it is for, organisation name, location, size, contact time, optional mobile number and notes) only to contact you about this audit, with your consent, and keep it in a private list seen by authorised team members. You can withdraw your interest at any time by emailing us; we then delete it [retention period for audit leads — CS/legal to set]. We do not share it with anyone else without asking you first.

8. Storage and security

Pledges are stored in a database controlled by Zero Cube [service provider to be confirmed — see SETUP]. Access is limited to authorised team members. We keep Wall of Honour data while you are listed and delete it on withdrawal or after [retention period — CS/legal to set].

9. Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data. Share buttons open the chosen social network; their own terms apply to what you post there.

10. Grievances

Questions or complaints: zerocubecif@gmail.com [grievance officer name — to be confirmed]. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational for your request.
