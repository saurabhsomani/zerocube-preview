Our core philosophy
Our core philosophy

वसुधैव कुटुम्बकम्

Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam: the world is one family.

An ancient Indian idea, shared as a value for everyone: every person, every living being and every child not yet born belongs to one planetary family. Whoever and wherever you are, when you join Zero Cube you become a Global Climate Citizen, with responsibilities to the planet and to each other.

  • To the planetLive lighter, protect nature, and measure honestly what changes.
  • To each otherShare what you know, include everyone, and leave no village or child behind.
  • To the futureLearn, teach and act for the generations who will inherit the earth. Contributes to SDG target 4.7.
One family, one planet, one future
Our Belief

One family, one planet, one future

We humans are the most intelligent species on this earth. With that gift comes a duty: to create a happy, equal world — not only for people, but for every living being — with the help of nature. Nature alone helps us grow and evolve, and it is nature that will make our future generations happy.

Our tradition says it simply: Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam — the world is one family. The rivers, forests, soils and creatures we share this earth with belong to that family, and so do the children not yet born. What we use today, we hold in trust for them.

Net Zero

is not only a compliance pledge. It is a way of living happily and healthily in harmony with nature — cleaner air, active days, wholesome food, less waste.

SROI

makes good measurable and trusted, so every rupee given for society shows the change it created.

Quality Education

is an equal right of every child — whatever their gender, caste, religion, income, location or ability.

NPOs

are society's changemakers. We help their work be seen, understood and supported.

This is not a mission for a few experts. It is from the people, for the people. Join us, from anywhere in the world: as a student, a teacher, a company, an NPO, a village, or simply as someone who cares.

Who we are

From the people, for the people

Zero Cube Climate & Social Impact Foundation was incorporated on 28 September 2026 as a not-for-profit company under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office in Pune. It is a not-for-profit company licensed under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 (Licence No. 192660). We are a people’s initiative: from the people, for the people. Our Board serve without remuneration, as philanthropists of time, expertise and networks. We have two main goals, as equals: Net Zero and climate action, and accessible, quality education for every child, unconditionally. We design frameworks and certify Net Zero journeys, are designing frameworks for schools to measure and raise education quality and equity, measure social value, run free learning for everyone, and help CSR and philanthropy reach NetZero villages and schools.

Change of this size cannot come from one organisation. It comes when people everywhere come together, and that is what Zero Cube exists to make possible.

Our dream

Every child on Earth deserves the same quality education, on a planet that can sustain them.

Mission

To design and steward open frameworks for climate action (Net Zero, ESG and SROI) and for quality and equal education (school infrastructure, teaching, and the support of industry and society), with certification programmes and CSR delivery models that turn them into lasting change at community scale.

Vision

A world where every child gets the same school foundation, teachers who shape responsible global citizens, and a society that helps them grow toward their own dream; and where NetZero villages and a green economy create dignified work for all.

Quality and equal education →

A child who learns well grows up to protect the planet. A planet in balance lets every child learn. That is why both are our main goals, side by side, as equals.

Unconditional: every child, no criteria. No fees, no marks or merit cut-offs, no conditions of income, caste, religion, gender, disability, language, location or nationality. Inequality begins when access has conditions, so ours has none. Our frameworks and certification criteria apply to institutions (schools, colleges, companies), never to children. Student membership is optional, for older students who want active roles: no child ever needs a membership or a payment to learn.

Our objects in brief
Objects

Our objects in brief

  • Design and steward ESG and Net Zero frameworks, criteria and certification programmes for villages, clubs, schools, MSMEs, events and organisations
  • Measure and report social and environmental value: SROI studies, impact assessments and dMRV
  • Climate and sustainability education and awareness: webinars, courses, school, college, teacher and youth programmes
  • Use CSR and philanthropic funds for NetZero villages, green rural livelihoods and quality education
  • Build the capacity of NGOs, trusts, panchayats and small enterprises to measure, report and raise funds for impact

Summary only; the Memorandum of Association is the governing text.

Philanthropists of time, expertise and networks

  • Time, expertise and networks — not money. Our directors give their time, expertise and networks voluntarily and serve without remuneration: no salary, sitting fee or other benefit beyond what the law and our Articles permit. Directors do not fund the company: no director loans, guarantees or liability, and spending follows income.
  • Membership or donations are voluntary. A director may join the programme at the normal published fee or make a voluntary donation. This is never a condition of being a director, is not a loan, and gives no liability or control.
  • Disclosed and reviewed. Any director membership or donation is recorded in the Board’s register of interests and in the minutes, and is reviewed by our Company Secretary and Chartered Accountant.
  • Nothing comes back. Donations go only to the Foundation’s objects, or directly to beneficiaries. No director receives any benefit or preferential service in return. A director’s personal giving to beneficiaries is separate and direct, and never passes through Zero Cube.

SDG target 4.7: education for global citizenship. Target 4.7 asks that every learner gains the knowledge and skills to promote sustainable development, including through education for sustainable lifestyles and global citizenship. That is the heart of our Global Climate Citizen idea: learning that turns into responsibility for the planet and for each other.
SDG 4: quality and equal education for every child. Our education goal, one of our two main goals, is built around target 4.1 (free, equitable and quality primary and secondary education for every girl and boy), 4.5 (eliminate disparities and ensure equal access), 4.a (safe, inclusive, effective schools), 4.c (more qualified teachers) and 4.4 (skills for decent work), with partnerships under SDG 17.

Zero Cube is an independent foundation; SDG references show alignment only and do not imply UN endorsement. Goals and targets: sdgs.un.org/goals.
