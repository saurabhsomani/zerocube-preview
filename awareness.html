How we deliver

Awareness & Training

Free public awareness, practical courses and readiness support for MSMEs.

In one line

Free webinars and practical courses help everyone take part.

Free public webinars

Two webinars a month on YouTube Live (and Zoom for interactive sessions), free for everyone. Every session stays online as a replay.

Planet Positive – Towards NetZero →

Zero Cube Climate Academy

Free short self-paced courses on climate basics, Net Zero for MSMEs, teaching, villages and SROI, with completion badges. Free for everyone; members also get members-only sessions twice a month.

Explore the Academy →

ESG & BRSR readiness for MSMEs

Learning series, templates and readiness diagnostics that help MSMEs understand and prepare the emissions and sustainability data large buyers increasingly request, including for BRSR value chains and CBAM-exposed exports. Educational and advisory support only — not assurance or a regulatory certification.
