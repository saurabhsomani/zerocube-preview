Be part of the change

Nine ways to take part, from anywhere in the world, each with clear steps. Every message to zerocubecif@gmail.com gets a personal reply.

In one line

Nine simple ways to be part of the change: join, volunteer, sponsor, partner, pledge or spread the word.

1

Join the movement as a Founding Member

Tell us how you would like to belong and contribute, from anywhere in the world. Choose your way of belonging and pay by bank transfer, UPI or online; learning stays free for everyone.

Email usDetails
2

Become a company member

Engage for 12 months, get endorsed by 2 members or 1 director, then orientation, interview and Board admission.

Email usDetails
3

Volunteer or join the Student Crew

Tell us your skills; we train you for webinar, field-data or village roles.

Email usDetails
4

Sponsor a webinar or a village

Choose webinar co-sponsorship or a NetZero village; we agree scope, budget and reporting.

Partner through CSR

Transparency

Where your money goes

As a Section 8 company, every rupee is applied only to our objects: no dividend or distribution is ever made, directors serve without remuneration, spending follows income and villages never pay. Each year we will publish a transparency report with our audited accounts, showing how income was actually received and applied.
