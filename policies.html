Policies

Our governance, safeguarding and privacy commitments.

In one line

How we behave, protect people and use data.

Code of conduct · Conflict of interest · Safeguarding · Privacy policy (summary; full text on the Privacy page) · Terms of use (summary; full text on the Terms page) · Donation & refund policy

Code of conduct

All directors, staff, advisors, members and volunteers act with honesty, respect and fairness; follow the law; protect confidential and personal information; avoid harassment and discrimination; and never use Zero Cube's name for personal gain. Breaches may lead to removal from roles or membership.

Draft, subject to Board approval

Conflict of interest

Anyone with a personal, family or financial interest in a decision must disclose it in writing and take no part in that decision. Directors disclose interests to the Board as required by law. Zero Cube never certifies or assesses a project it designed or implemented, and reviewers must be free of conflicts. A register of interests is maintained.

Draft, subject to Board approval

Safeguarding

We protect children and vulnerable adults in all programmes. Schools give written consent; parents consent before any child's data or photo is used; minors are not named publicly; under-18s work only under school or guardian supervision; concerns are reported to the designated safeguarding lead and acted on promptly.

Draft, subject to Board approval

Privacy policy (summary; full text on the Privacy page)

We collect only the personal data needed to respond to you, manage membership, donations and programmes, and we process it in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. We do not sell data or use third-party trackers. You may ask to access, correct or delete your data by emailing us.

Draft, subject to Board approval

Terms of use (summary; full text on the Terms page)

Content on this site is provided for information. Certificates, reports and figures are valid only as described in their published scope. Zero Cube does not endorse third-party sites linked from this site. Use of our name or marks requires written permission.

Draft, subject to Board approval

Donation & refund policy

Zero Cube is not accepting donations or membership payments yet; they open only after our bank account is active. When they open: donations are voluntary and are applied to our objects or to the purpose the donor specifies. Donations are accepted in INR from Indian sources only; Zero Cube does not seek donations from outside India. International supporters take part through membership, and international membership payments open only after the applicable approvals and banking are in place. Donations made in error may be refunded on written request within 15 days, net of charges. Membership fees are refundable only as stated in the membership terms. Tax receipts are issued only for eligible donations after the relevant approvals.

Draft, subject to Board approval
