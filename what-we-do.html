What we do

What we do

Two equal goals: a cleaner planet (Net Zero) and a good education for every child.

In one line

Two equal goals: a cleaner planet, and a good education for every child, with no conditions.

In plain words

  • We check, guide and certify schools, villages, businesses, NPOs and people as they cut pollution.
  • We work for a good, equal education for every child.
  • Both goals are equal. Villages never pay.
Plain words
Net Zero
cutting the gases that heat our planet, and balancing the rest by giving back to nature
dMRV
proof tracking: digital records of what changed, checked by human experts
SROI
a report that shows the good done, in rupees, for every ₹1 spent
Scope 3
the carbon from a company’s suppliers, transport and products
CSR
money some companies must, by law, spend on social good

Net Zero and climate action · Quality education for every child

Two main goals, as equals, with SROI measuring the change in both.

Main goal: Net Zero and climate action

Scope 3 value-chain assessments for corporates, footprinting and training for MSME vendors, certification under our criteria — and Net Zero as a happier, healthier way to live.

Main goal: Quality education for every child

Unconditional: every child, no criteria. Education is a fundamental right. Every child deserves an equal opportunity to learn — whatever their gender, caste, religion, income, location or ability — with moral responsibility towards the climate.

Learn more →

SROI — Social Return on Investment

We measure the social, environmental and economic value created for every ₹1 invested, following the Principles of Social Value.

Learn more →

Unconditional: every child, no criteria. No fees, no marks or merit cut-offs, no conditions of income, caste, religion, gender, disability, language, location or nationality. Inequality begins when access has conditions, so ours has none. Our frameworks and certification criteria apply to institutions (schools, colleges, companies), never to children. Student membership is optional, for older students who want active roles: no child ever needs a membership or a payment to learn.

SDG target 4.7: education for global citizenship. Target 4.7 asks that every learner gains the knowledge and skills to promote sustainable development, including through education for sustainable lifestyles and global citizenship. That is the heart of our Global Climate Citizen idea: learning that turns into responsibility for the planet and for each other.
SDG 4: quality and equal education for every child. Our education goal, one of our two main goals, is built around target 4.1 (free, equitable and quality primary and secondary education for every girl and boy), 4.5 (eliminate disparities and ensure equal access), 4.a (safe, inclusive, effective schools), 4.c (more qualified teachers) and 4.4 (skills for decent work), with partnerships under SDG 17.

Zero Cube is an independent foundation; SDG references show alignment only and do not imply UN endorsement. Goals and targets: sdgs.un.org/goals.

Main goal · Net Zero and climate action

Explore →

Why it matters

Most corporate emissions sit in the value chain (Scope 3), much of it with MSME suppliers — and low-carbon living is also healthier living.

What we do

Scope 3 readiness and assessments for corporate supply chains, MSME vendor footprinting and training, employee commuting and lifestyle engagement, certification under our criteria and CSR-funded NetZero villages.

Who benefits

Gram Panchayats and villages, clubs, MSMEs and their buyers, schools and campuses.

How members take part

Certify your organisation, sponsor a village, join as an MSME or Eco-Product member, volunteer for field data.

Main goal · Accessible, quality education for every child

Explore →

Why it matters

Every child on Earth deserves the same quality education. Education is a fundamental right (Article 21A, RTE Act 2009), yet inequality begins in the first classroom.

What we do

Frameworks being designed to measure and raise education quality and equity across school infrastructure, teaching, and the support of industry and society; Carbon Detectives school audits, the Net Zero School pathway and assessments, teacher fellowship, free Climate Academy courses and the active student pathway.

Who benefits

Students, teachers, government and low-fee schools, colleges.

How members take part

Join as a student or institution, bring the programme to your school, mentor, sponsor a school.

How we measure both · SROI

Explore →

Why it matters

NPOs do society's work in line with the SDGs; donors and funders need to see, in measurable terms, what really changed.

What we do

SROI studies, CSR impact assessments, SROI forecasts and village and school SROI reports following the Principles of Social Value.

Who benefits

CSR teams, foundations, NPOs, schools and villages.

How members take part

Commission a study, list a verified project as an NPO, take an SROI course, contribute data.
